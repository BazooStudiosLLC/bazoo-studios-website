Bazoo Studios LLC respects your privacy. This policy explains what information this website handles and the choices available to you.

01 Information we collect

This coming-soon website does not ask you to create an account, submit a form, or provide personal information directly to Bazoo Studios LLC. We do not currently use advertising cookies, behavioral tracking, or analytics scripts.

02 Hosting information

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like most hosting providers, GitHub may automatically process technical information—such as an IP address, browser type, device information, and request timestamps—to deliver and secure the service. GitHub’s handling of that information is governed by its own privacy statement.

03 Cookies and local storage

Bazoo Studios LLC does not currently place cookies or use browser storage on this website. Third-party infrastructure used to deliver the site may operate under its own policies.

04 Children’s privacy

This website is a general-audience studio announcement and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If we learn that personal information from a child was collected through a future feature, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

05 Future services

As Bazoo Studios LLC introduces games, newsletters, community features, or other services, those experiences may require additional privacy disclosures. We will update this policy before new data practices take effect and will revise the date shown above.

06 Your choices

Because this site does not currently collect information directly, there is no account or marketing subscription to manage here. You may control browser settings or discontinue use of the site at any time.

07 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the website, our practices, or applicable requirements. The “Last updated” date will identify the latest version.

08 Contact

Questions about this privacy policy may be directed to Bazoo Studios LLC through the official contact channel published with our future studio services.
