Privacy
Policy.

Bazoo Studios LLC respects your privacy. This policy explains what information this website handles and the choices available to you.

01 Information we collect

If you create a Bazoo Player account, we receive identity information supplied through Google, such as your name, email address, account identifier, and profile image. We also store profile details you choose to provide, connected-account status, points, ranks, achievements, and verified game scores. We do not currently use advertising cookies or behavioral advertising.

02 Hosting information

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like most hosting providers, GitHub may automatically process technical information—such as an IP address, browser type, device information, and request timestamps—to deliver and secure the service. GitHub’s handling of that information is governed by its own privacy statement.

03 Cookies and local storage

The Player Hub uses necessary browser storage to keep you signed in securely. Google provides sign-in and Supabase provides authentication and database services; those providers process information under their own policies. These technologies are used for account operation, security, and fraud prevention rather than advertising.

04 Children’s privacy

The website and Player Hub are general-audience services and are not directed to children under 13. If we learn that personal information from a child was collected without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

05 How information is used

We use account information to operate player profiles, points, ranks, leaderboards, connected-account badges, game integrations, security, and support. Public leaderboards may display your chosen display name, username, avatar, rank, and points. Your email address and private account identifiers are not shown on public leaderboards.

06 Your choices

You may edit your profile and disconnect by signing out. To request access, correction, or deletion of account information, contact us using the address below. Social connections and rewards may be removed or adjusted when verification expires, an account is disconnected, or abuse is detected.

07 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the website, our practices, or applicable requirements. The “Last updated” date will identify the latest version.

08 Contact

Questions about this privacy policy may be sent to dakota.folger@bazoostudios.com.
